### 👨‍🔬 About Me
I am a **Senior Research Scientist at DrongoAI**, where I bridge the gap between theoretical signal processing and clinical AI. My work focuses on solving complex **inverse problems** in medical imaging to enhance diagnostic accuracy and patient safety.

* **Current Focus:** Developing classical and deep learning architectures for **X-ray disease screening** and high-throughput medical vision systems.
* **Post-Doctoral Research:** Served as an **ANRF (Govt. of India) NPDF Scholar at IIT Kharagpur**, specializing in MRI reconstruction and low-dose CT denoising.
* **Education:** Ph.D. from **NIT Rourkela**, with a deep dive into image restoration and computer vision.

---

### 🛠 Technical Expertise
* **Computer Vision:** Image Restoration, Feature Extraction, and Disease Detection.
* **Medical Imaging:** MRI Reconstruction, CT Denoising, and Radiographic Analysis.
* **Core Math:** Deep Learning for Inverse Problems, Optimization, and Signal Processing.

> **Research Philosophy:** I aim to recover high-fidelity latent images $x$ from noisy or undersampled observations $y$ by modeling:
> $$y = \mathcal{A}(x) + \eta$$
> where $\mathcal{A}$ represents the forward imaging operator and $\eta$ is the acquisition noise.
